This is a human-written description of the Faust-Studio project. 

Client website: https://www.fauststudio.net/
Client Company: Faust Studio, a recording studio in Prague, Czechia
Product type: Audio technology / digital product marketing project

Project Description: This project supports the web-based vocal autotuning tool developed by a separate Project team. Me and my team will focus on how the product is positioned, presented, and promoted to users through a landing page and an AI agent or marketing automation.

# Our Team's Key Deliverables:

The project is to create the product landing page, develop a marketing strategy, and build an AI agent or automation that implements one of the selected marketing ideas.

• Competitive review of similar tools.
• Design and build a landing page for the vocal autotuning tool.
• Develop a marketing strategy for the product.
• Design and build an AI agent or automation based on the marketing strategy.
• QA/UX testing, documentation, and recommendations for future development.

# Ultimate Project Goals:
Direct potential clients towards the autotune application (henceforth "the product") and convert clients of the product into paying customers (the product is free) via upselling music courses, studio time at Faust Studio, and other Faust Studio products. 

The autotune tool will require an email signup to authenticate users, and those emails will be collected in a central location for us to reach out to later to advertise and promote the products mentioned in the previous paragraph. 


Current development platform for the website (before release to a domain): Github Pages
