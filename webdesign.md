
## Rough ideas
 
Every free autotune site we looked at looks like a generic utility, so a warmer, vintage look is an easy way for us to stand out. If the page looks and feels like that place, we can make the jump from the free tool to booking a session feel natural.
  
## Inspiration
 
- [Abbey Road](https://www.abbeyroad.com/the-studios): how they present rooms and services. Our "next step" section should feel like this.
- [LA Studio](https://la-studio.cc/en/autotune): closest to our "try it" idea. Free to use, account only for more.
- [Soundtrap](https://www.soundtrap.com/musicmakers): short hero, simple feature blocks, sample tracks to try.
- [RysUpTune](https://rysupaudio.com/products/rysuptune): strength presets (Natural to Hard Tune), before/after player. I like their plugin explanation.
- (https://rysupaudio.com/pages/vocal-tuner): also their vocal tuner
- [remove.bg](https://www.remove.bg/): the whole hero is one upload box.
## Principles
 
- People can try the tool within seconds of landing.
- Be clear about what's free and when we ask for an email.
- Czech and English from the start.
## Try first, email at download
 
At least five free autotune sites need no account, so an email wall up front will make people leave.
 
1. Upload or record a vocal
2. Tune it and listen for free
3. Enter email to download
4. Show "book a session" / "take a course"
Marketing emails = a separate checkbox (GDPR).
 
Needs to be agreed with the tool team, since they own the signup.
